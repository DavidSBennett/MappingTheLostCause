    About this project

    In 1939, timed to the Atlanta premiere of Gone with the Wind, a campaign placed dozens of historical markers across Atlanta commemorating the 1864 campaign and the city's early history. This project plots those markers on the 1945 Land Use Map of Atlanta, whose legend — including its explicitly racialized housing categories — is itself a primary source for how the city saw itself at the moment the Lost Cause was being fixed into the landscape.

    Click a numbered pin to read the archival marker text, drawn from Wilbur Kurtz's descriptions and contemporary newspaper accounts.
